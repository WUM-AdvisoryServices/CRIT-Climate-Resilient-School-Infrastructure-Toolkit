Flagship toolkit

Climate-Resilient School Infrastructure Toolkit

CRIT · CC0 1.0

An open-source, scalable framework for protecting learning environments in climate-vulnerable regions — beginning in Pakistan — against floods, heatwaves, and extreme rain. Governments, engineers, education planners, humanitarian actors, and communities can adapt it freely.

  1. 01

    Climate Risk & Vulnerability Screening

    Hazard screening and a school vulnerability score so scarce retrofit budgets reach classrooms most exposed to floods, heatwaves, and extreme rain.

  2. 02

    Modular Design & Retrofitting Checklists

    Adaptable templates for raised plinths, drainage, passive cooling, storm strengthening, and costing — built to be localised, not copied wholesale.

  3. 03

    Community Engagement & Maintenance Materials

    Training agendas and ownership tools for district teams, school committees, parents, and students so a safer school stays maintained.

Current status, in the project’s own words: advanced prototype. Screening, retrofitting guidance, costing templates, and a training deck are drafted. Field-testing and demonstration retrofits — on the order of 10 to 15 schools — come after district approval and technical partners. Nothing on this page treats a planned pilot as a completed result.

Open source instruments

Fill the toolkit, don’t just download it

Fifteen field forms drawn from the public CRIT repository. They score, total, and export in the browser. CC0 — use them, change them, and keep the sheet.

01 · Risk

  • Screen the school

    Climate risk screening and prioritisation score

    Score hazard, the building, and the community. The composite band follows the published CRIT screen: 0–10 low, 11–20 medium, 21–30 high, 31 and above critical.

    Open form

  • Walk the site

    School vulnerability assessment checklist

    The field checklist from Module 01: history, site, structure, heat, flood, WASH, power, inclusion, and preparedness. A No on a safety item is counted below.

    Open form

  • One-page field card

    Rapid school infrastructure assessment

    The pocket form for a district visit: four risk levels, observed damage, child safety, photos, and the intervention you would recommend today.

    Open form

02 · Design

  • Pass the drawing gate

    Technical drawings submission checklist

    Seven approval checks from the Module 02 drawing list. A package is not ready until the plinth, drainage, roof anchors, inclusion, girls' toilets, and the BOQ agree.

    Open form

03 · Retrofit

  • Build the bill

    Retrofitting bill of quantities

    Line items from the Module 03 BOQ. Quantity times unit cost is the line total. Add labour in the same sheet.

    Open form

  • Keep the site safe

    Site safety and construction quality checklist

    For engineers, supervisors, and contractors. Any No is a hold point until it is corrected. Child safeguarding is not optional.

    Open form

04 · Cost

  • Price the package

    Costing calculator

    District rates in, package totals out. Supervision is 5 percent and contingency is 10 percent, as in the CRIT calculator. Cost per child uses the enrolment you enter.

    Open form

  • Weigh five years

    Preventive retrofit versus repeated repairs

    The Module 04 comparison: preventive cost is the retrofit plus annual upkeep. Reactive cost is disaster repairs times how often they hit, plus yearly patching. This is a planning sketch, not an appraisal.

    Open form

  • Buy it clean

    Procurement plan

    Goods, works, and services against a ceiling. Methods match the template: direct purchase, quotation, competitive bid, framework, or a government vendor list.

    Open form

05 · Training

  • Fill the room, then the review

    Training attendance, pre/post test, and feedback

    One session, three sheets from the Module 05 workbook. Sample questions are open prompts from the toolkit — replace them with your own paper.

    Open form

06 · Pilot

  • Pick the pilot

    Pilot school selection matrix

    Four scores from 0 to 3. The composite is their sum. Rank 1 is the highest score — the school the matrix would visit first. Flood and heat figures below are from the public sample; infrastructure condition and poverty are left for your survey.

    Open form

  • Track the change

    Monitoring, baseline, and progress

    The three sheets in the Module 06 framework. Sample indicators are the toolkit's own examples, including targets that are still a plan, not a result.

    Open form

07 · Sample data

  • Open the register

    School profile data

    The public sample register: five schools from the CRIT workbook. Edit them, add your own, and download a sheet. These rows are demonstration data, not a verified census.

    Open form

  • Count the closures

    Climate closure-days baseline

    Days a school could not hold classes because of flood, heat, or storm. The sample year is 2024 from the public workbook. Totals add the three causes — the original sheet left some totals at zero.

    Open form

  • Pin the map

    Risk map sheet for My Maps

    The minimum columns from the risk-map note: name, latitude, longitude, and a risk level. Download the CSV and import it into Google My Maps, then style by risk level.

    Open form
